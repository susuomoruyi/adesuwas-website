Some products market themselves. If you sell sneakers, food, or skincare, people already want to see whatever you post.

[Accrue](https://useaccrue.com/) helps individuals and businesses send money from one African country to another. There’s nothing fun about me telling you to send money from Ghana to Nigeria. The natural way to market it is to say that over and over again, and it’s boring. People don’t like boring.

So for a while now, my marketing team and I have been thinking about it differently. We’re a media company that does finance.

To be fair, we’re not actually a media company. But that’s how we like to think of ourselves, as opposed to a finance company that uses media to push its product.

They sound like the same thing, but they’re not. A finance company using media ends up with some version of “use Accrue to send money” in every post. A media company that does finance starts by asking what people would actually want to watch or read, and then finds a way to connect it back to us. When it works, people like the brand before they ever download the app.

Here’s what that’s looked like for us:

**1. Noodles vs. a transfer**

We recently started doing comparison videos. Which is faster: cooking noodles, or sending money from Ghana to Nigeria? There’s a timer. One person is cooking & sending money, and you just want to see which finishes first. It’s entertaining, and it shows how fast the product is without us having to say “we’re fast.”

**2. Friday quizzes**

Every Friday we post a quiz where you can test your finance knowledge. For example, can you guess the country from its currency? On a good Friday, over a thousand people take it. The end screen is where we point people back to Accrue.

**3. Side hustles, not “3 easy steps”**

For SEO, we could write “How to send money from Ghana to Nigeria in 3 easy steps” forever. Instead, we write things like how to make an extra $100 a month, or side hustles for 9-to-5ers. Somewhere in the middle, say in the part about finding clients on Upwork, we mention that you can open a dollar account on Accrue to get paid, or use Accrue to work with clients across Africa. We never lead with “here’s how to receive money.” We lead with what the person actually wants.

These are just a few examples. The product is in every one of these, but It’s just not the headline.

So yeah, if your product is boring, start thinking of yourself as a media company that sells XYZ, and you’ll see how it radically changes how you approach social media.
